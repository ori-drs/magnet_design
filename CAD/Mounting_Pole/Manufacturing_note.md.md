This document provides technical guidance for manufacturing the Magnet mounting pole used in field data collection with the ORI Magnet LiDAR mapping device.  
The pole assembly consists of two sections: the Magnet holder and the pole body.

## 1. Magnet Holder

The Magnet holder is a modular structure designed to secure the Magnet unit and allow adjustable orientation. It consists of the following components:

- **Magnet mounting plate:** The primary platform for securing the Magnet using M4 screws at the Tappex mounting points.  
- **Tilt side panel:** Provides the angle adjustment mechanism and additional structural support between the mounting plate and the mast cap.  
- **Mast cap:** Connects the holder assembly to the carbon fibre pole via a threaded insert.

*Notes*
*- These component should be 3D-printed in a material (e.g., PLA or PETG) with at least 30% infill for strength.*
*- The tilt adjustment mechanism is recommanded to be manufactured from an aluminium plate via CNC machining for rigidity*


## 2. Pole Body

The pole body is made from a carbon fibre (CF) tube with an outer diameter of 30 mm.  
The inner diameter and wall thickness are not critical, as long as the tube maintains adequate structural integrity.

- The length of the CF tube can be adjusted depending on the application.  
- In the current ORI design, the CF tube includes a [CF insert with an M10 internal thread](https://www.accu.co.uk/metal-threaded-inserts-standard/588069-HMTIR-30-M10-HDPE-BL), which connects the mast cap for modular assembly and additional CF tube segments using M10 screws for extension.
